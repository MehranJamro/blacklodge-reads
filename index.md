# The Black Lodge Reading Space

Most book-clubs are created only for casual readings. We found a dearth of communal spaces that approach books usually considered dense and effortful. It’s a hundred times more difficult to trudge through Satantango, or The Anatomy of Melancholy, without the scaffolding and support of a community. That’s why we have created an online reading-club that aims to tackle hard literature together, as a community. 

The Black Lodge Reading Space aims to be more worthwhile to you than a mere discussion-group, therefore, we will also be offering:
- hand-made (non-AI) resources for each book 
- actionable cheat-sheets (on friction, comprehension, consistency, existential crises, and the like)
- live sessions with invited scholars and experts 
- a monthly digest for all participants with contextual analyses and supplementary material.

## Why a Walled Garden?
This book-club will not be open to all and requires you to fill a registration form to be considered. Why keep a walled-garden? Because we want our space to be highly-curated and offer each member the best experience we can. We don’t want to offer the same perks a mere sub-reddit or Youtube comment-section already offers. 

## Our Vision
We are all victims of the travesty that is our education system. We were systematically dumbed down, shoved into a fucked up, shame-based rat-race that has fostered lifelong resentments toward ‘hard reads’. We aim to process those feelings together and sever those negative associations through the warmth of a community.

With the inescapable distractions of doomscrolling, brain-rot, the destruction of the traditional communal space, we need to, more than ever, take back the most hijacked ‘commodities’ of our era: our time and our capacity to read deeply.

We are not anti-pleasure, though: We aren’t primarily an academic-oriented book club (although we welcome in-depth analyses!). We want each reading to leave us mind-blown. We aim to leave each meeting different from how we entered. 

## Who it’s meant for:
- People particularly interested in a classical education. We will be 
- Those who want a judgement-free, non-pretentious pro-yapping space about dense texts.
- Survivors of education systems processing their resentments
